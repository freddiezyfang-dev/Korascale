# Brand Positioning

KoraScale is a China travel design and operations partner — not a discount tour aggregator or generic content site.

## Primary audiences

1. International travelers planning a trip to China.
2. Overseas teams visiting China for business.
3. Chinese companies hosting overseas clients, partners, or executives.
4. Overseas travel agencies seeking a China ground partner.

## Core content scope

- China travel planning
- Destination selection and route strategy
- Local culture, dining, and experiences
- Private small-group and luxury journeys
- Group tours
- China corporate travel, business visits, exhibitions, conferences, customer events
- Post-event bleisure and VIP client hosting

## What the site is not

- A low-price product listing site
- A generic attraction encyclopedia
- A philosophical culture magazine
- An OTA
- A news outlet

## Editorial implication

Articles should demonstrate practical judgment for international readers. Service mentions support the reader's decision — they do not replace useful answers.
