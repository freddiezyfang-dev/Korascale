# Writing Style

## Tone

- Clear and direct
- Editorial, not salesy
- Restrained — show judgment, not hype
- Friendly to international readers unfamiliar with China logistics
- Answer the reader's question before introducing KoraScale services

## Avoid

- Philosophical titles that hide the real search question
- Abstract concepts without practical payoff
- Empty brand language
- Keyword stuffing
- Excessive rhetorical questions
- Overuse of em dashes
- Empty marketing words: *transformative*, *unforgettable*, *authentic*, *journey of a lifetime*
- Invented statistics, policies, visa rules, prices, opening hours, train schedules, client stories, or service capabilities
- Promises about search rankings or traffic

## Good patterns

- Lead with the decision or problem the reader has
- Use concrete nouns (city, route, season, visa type, meeting format)
- Short paragraphs; scannable headings
- When uncertain, flag in `factCheckItems` instead of guessing
